Void an invoice when it needs to be canceled. It remains in your records with the status `voided`. To remove a draft invoice, [delete it instead](/v2/guide/invoicing/draft-invoices#delete-a-draft-invoice).

<Warning>
Voiding cannot be undone. Review any refund or credit amounts before confirming.
</Warning>

## Void an invoice

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Dashboard">
    1. Open **Invoices**.
    2. Click the invoice’s **ellipsis icon**.
    3. Select **Void invoice**.
    4. Choose whether to generate a credit note.
    5. Review any refund or credit amounts.
    6. Confirm.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="API">
    <CodeGroup>
    ```bash Void an invoice
    LAGO_URL="https://api.getlago.com"
    API_KEY="__YOUR_API_KEY__"
    INVOICE_ID="__YOUR_INVOICE_ID__"

    curl --location --request POST "$LAGO_URL/api/v2/invoices/$INVOICE_ID/void" \
      --header "Authorization: Bearer $API_KEY"
    ```
    </CodeGroup>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Void and generate a credit note

When voiding an invoice, you can also generate a credit note to refund a payment or give your customer credit for future invoices.

<Info>
Generating a credit note while voiding requires a premium license. Voiding without a credit note does not.
</Info>

The request body accepts three optional fields:

| Field | Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `generate_credit_note` | boolean | Whether to generate credit notes. |
| `refund_amount` | integer, cents | Amount to refund. |
| `credit_amount` | integer, cents | Amount to credit to the customer. |

### Example

This example requests a USD 100 refund and USD 44 in credit for a fully paid USD 144 invoice. The invoice for `contract_example` includes a USD 120 subtotal and 20% tax.

<CodeGroup>
```bash Void with a refund and a credit
LAGO_URL="https://api.getlago.com"
API_KEY="__YOUR_API_KEY__"
INVOICE_ID="__YOUR_INVOICE_ID__"

curl --location --request POST "$LAGO_URL/api/v2/invoices/$INVOICE_ID/void" \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer $API_KEY" \
  --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --data-raw '{
    "generate_credit_note": true,
    "refund_amount": 10000,
    "credit_amount": 4400
  }'
```
</CodeGroup>

The body is not wrapped in an `invoice` key. The resulting credit note follows the rules in [Credit notes](/v2/guide/invoicing/credit-notes/overview).
