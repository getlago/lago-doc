Voiding an invoice cancels it while keeping it on record with a `voided` status and a `voided_at` timestamp. Use it when a finalized invoice should not be collected, for example after a billing error, and you need an auditable trail.

A voided invoice rejects updates. To remove a `draft` invoice instead, [delete it](/v2/guide/invoicing/draft-invoices#delete-a-draft-invoice).

<Warning>
Voiding changes the financial record of the invoice and cannot be undone through the API. If you also generate a credit note, a refund can be sent to the customer's payment method. Check the invoice identifier and the amounts, and review the change before you run it in production.
</Warning>

## Void an invoice

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Dashboard">
    1. Open the **Invoices** list;
    2. Click the **ellipsis icon** on the invoice line;
    3. Select **Void invoice**; and
    4. Choose the refund option, then confirm.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="API">
    <CodeGroup>
    ```bash Void an invoice
    LAGO_URL="https://api.getlago.com"
    API_KEY="__YOUR_API_KEY__"
    INVOICE_ID="__YOUR_INVOICE_ID__"

    curl --location --request POST "$LAGO_URL/api/v2/invoices/$INVOICE_ID/void" \
      --header "Authorization: Bearer $API_KEY"
    ```
    </CodeGroup>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Void and generate a credit note

<Info>
Generating a credit note while voiding requires a premium license. Voiding without a credit note does not.
</Info>

The request body accepts three optional fields:

| Field | Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `generate_credit_note` | boolean | Whether to generate credit notes. |
| `refund_amount` | integer, cents | Amount to refund. |
| `credit_amount` | integer, cents | Amount to credit to the customer. |

### Example

The `contract_example` invoice totals $144.00 ($120.00 plus 20% tax) and was paid in full. You void it, refund $100.00 and credit the remaining $44.00 for future invoices:

<CodeGroup>
```bash Void with a refund and a credit
LAGO_URL="https://api.getlago.com"
API_KEY="__YOUR_API_KEY__"
INVOICE_ID="__YOUR_INVOICE_ID__"

curl --location --request POST "$LAGO_URL/api/v2/invoices/$INVOICE_ID/void" \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer $API_KEY" \
  --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --data-raw '{
    "generate_credit_note": true,
    "refund_amount": 10000,
    "credit_amount": 4400
  }'
```
</CodeGroup>

The body is not wrapped in an `invoice` key. The resulting credit note follows the rules in [Credit notes](/v2/guide/invoicing/credit-notes/overview).
