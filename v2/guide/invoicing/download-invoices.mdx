Download a finalized invoice as a PDF to share with your customer or keep for your records. You can also resend the invoice email through the API.

## Download an invoice PDF

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Dashboard">
    1. Click **Customers**.
    2. Select a customer.
    3. Open the **Invoices** tab.
    4. Click the invoice’s **ellipsis icon**.
    5. Click **Download invoice**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="API">
    Request the PDF with `POST /invoices/{lago_id}/download_pdf`. The invoice must be finalized.

    <CodeGroup>
    ```bash Download an invoice PDF
    LAGO_URL="https://api.getlago.com"
    API_KEY="__YOUR_API_KEY__"
    INVOICE_ID="__YOUR_INVOICE_ID__"

    curl --location --request POST "$LAGO_URL/api/v2/invoices/$INVOICE_ID/download_pdf" \
      --header "Authorization: Bearer $API_KEY"
    ```
    </CodeGroup>

    If the PDF is ready, the request returns `200` with the invoice and its `pdf_url`. Use this URL to download the file.

    If Lago needs to generate the PDF, the request returns `202` with an empty body. [Retrieve the invoice](/v2/api-reference/invoices/retrieve) later and use `pdf_url` when it becomes available.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Resend an invoice email

Resend a finalized invoice when your customer needs another copy. Email delivery must be configured on the [billing entity](/v2/guide/billing-entities), and this action requires a premium license.

Check the recipients before sending. If `to` is omitted or empty, Lago uses the customer’s email address.

Send `POST /invoices/{lago_id}/resend_email` with any of these optional fields:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `to` | Recipients. When omitted or empty, Lago uses the customer email. |
| `cc` | Additional recipients. |
| `bcc` | Additional recipients. |

<CodeGroup>
```bash Resend an invoice email
LAGO_URL="https://api.getlago.com"
API_KEY="__YOUR_API_KEY__"
INVOICE_ID="__YOUR_INVOICE_ID__"

curl --location --request POST "$LAGO_URL/api/v2/invoices/$INVOICE_ID/resend_email" \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer $API_KEY" \
  --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --data-raw '{
    "to": ["billing@example.com"],
    "cc": ["finance@example.com"]
  }'
```
</CodeGroup>

A successful request queues the email. It does not confirm delivery.
