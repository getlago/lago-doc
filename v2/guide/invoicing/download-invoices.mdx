Lago renders a PDF for each finalized invoice so you can send it to your customer or archive it. Use the download endpoint to get the file, and the resend endpoint to email it again.

## Download an invoice PDF

`POST /invoices/{lago_id}/download_pdf` works for finalized invoices only:

- If the PDF already exists, the response is `200` with the invoice. Read `pdf_url`.
- Otherwise, Lago queues PDF generation and returns `202` with an empty body. Call the endpoint again, or retrieve the invoice later and read `pdf_url`.

`pdf_url` and `xml_url` are `null` while the document is unavailable.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Dashboard">
    1. Click **Customers** in the side menu;
    2. Select a customer and open the **Invoices** tab;
    3. Click the **ellipsis icon** on the invoice; and
    4. Click **Download invoice**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="API">
    <CodeGroup>
    ```bash Download an invoice PDF
    LAGO_URL="https://api.getlago.com"
    API_KEY="__YOUR_API_KEY__"
    INVOICE_ID="__YOUR_INVOICE_ID__"

    curl --location --request POST "$LAGO_URL/api/v2/invoices/$INVOICE_ID/download_pdf" \
      --header "Authorization: Bearer $API_KEY"
    ```
    </CodeGroup>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Resend an invoice email

<Info>
Resending an invoice email requires a premium license, valid recipients and an email configuration on the [billing entity](/v2/guide/billing-entities).
</Info>

`POST /invoices/{lago_id}/resend_email` queues an email for a finalized invoice. All body fields are optional:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `to` | Recipients. When omitted or empty, Lago uses the customer email. |
| `cc` | Additional recipients. |
| `bcc` | Additional recipients. |

<Warning>
This sends an email to your customer. Check the recipients before you call it in production.
</Warning>

<CodeGroup>
```bash Resend an invoice email
LAGO_URL="https://api.getlago.com"
API_KEY="__YOUR_API_KEY__"
INVOICE_ID="__YOUR_INVOICE_ID__"

curl --location --request POST "$LAGO_URL/api/v2/invoices/$INVOICE_ID/resend_email" \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer $API_KEY" \
  --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --data-raw '{
    "to": ["billing@example.com"],
    "cc": ["finance@example.com"]
  }'
```
</CodeGroup>
