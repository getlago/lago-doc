A rate model defines how a rate turns a measured or configured quantity into an amount. Set it on each [rate](/v2/guide/rate-cards/rates) with `rate_model` and configure it with `rate_properties`, after checking below that the model fits your product type, billable metric and billing timing.

## Model comparison

| Rate model | `rate_model` | How it prices | Main `rate_properties` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Standard](/v2/guide/rate-cards/pricing-models/standard) | `standard` | One unit price for every unit | `amount` |
| [Graduated](/v2/guide/rate-cards/pricing-models/graduated) | `graduated` | Each tier prices its own slice of the quantity | `graduated_ranges` |
| [Volume](/v2/guide/rate-cards/pricing-models/volume) | `volume` | The total quantity picks one tier for all units | `volume_ranges` |
| [Package](/v2/guide/rate-cards/pricing-models/package) | `package` | A fixed amount per bundle of units | `amount`, `package_size` |
| [Percentage](/v2/guide/rate-cards/pricing-models/percentage) | `percentage` | A percentage of a measured monetary amount | `rate`, `fixed_amount` |
| [Graduated percentage](/v2/guide/rate-cards/pricing-models/graduated-percentage) | `graduated_percentage` | Percentage tiers on a measured monetary amount | `graduated_percentage_ranges` |
| [Dynamic](/v2/guide/rate-cards/pricing-models/dynamic) | `dynamic` | The amount comes from each event's `precise_total_amount_cents` | None |

The API enum also lists `custom`. It is not covered by this documentation.

Tiered models share one tier shape, `RateTierInput`: `to_value` (upper bound, decimal string, `null` on the last open-ended tier), `per_unit_amount`, `flat_amount` and `rate`. Upper bounds must increase. Do not send `from_value`.

## Model availability

A model can be available for one combination of product type, aggregation, `recurring`, proration and billing timing, and unavailable for another. Each cell reads **arrears / advance**. ✅ means available, ❌ means unavailable. "n/a" means the row has no separate proration setting.

Aggregation is the billable metric's `aggregation_type`: `count_agg`, `unique_count_agg`, `latest_agg`, `max_agg`, `sum_agg`, `weighted_sum_agg`. Proration is the rate card's `proration`.

### Fixed products

Fixed products use configured quantities. They have no billable metric and no `recurring` setting.

| Proration | Standard | Graduated | Volume | Package | Percentage | Graduated percentage | Dynamic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Full (`false`) | ✅ / ✅ | ✅ / ✅ | ✅ / ❌ | ❌ / ❌ | ❌ / ❌ | ❌ / ❌ | ❌ / ❌ |
| Prorated (`true`) | ✅ / ✅ | ✅ / ❌ | ✅ / ❌ | ❌ / ❌ | ❌ / ❌ | ❌ / ❌ | ❌ / ❌ |

In short: fixed products support `standard`, `graduated` and `volume`. `volume` is never billed in advance. `dynamic` requires a SUM metric.

### Related options

| Option | Field | Availability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spending minimum | Rate `min_amount_cents` | Arrears only. Unavailable with advance billing. See [spending minimums](/v2/guide/rate-cards/spending-minimums). |
| Display on invoice | Rate card `display_on_invoice` | Listed for advance billing. |
| Regroup paid fees | Rate card `regroup_paid_fees` | Only for hidden advance fees (`display_on_invoice: false`). |

## Example

The running example uses `standard` twice: $100 per month for one fixed unit and $0.01 per API call. A full month with 2,000 calls bills $100.00 + $20.00 = $120.00 before tax. The `api_calls` metric is a non-recurring COUNT on an arrears rate card, so every model except `dynamic` would be available for it.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which rate model should I start with?">
    `standard`, unless the price per unit changes with quantity. Use `graduated` when each tier prices its own slice, `volume` when the total quantity picks one price for all units, and `package` when you sell bundles.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between graduated and volume?">
    With 250 units and tiers at $1.00 up to 100, $0.50 up to 200, $0.10 above: graduated bills 100 × $1.00 + 100 × $0.50 + 50 × $0.10 = $155. Volume picks the tier that contains 250 and bills all 250 units at $0.10 = $25, plus that tier's flat fee if it has one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is a model rejected for my rate card?">
    Availability depends on the product type, the metric aggregation and `recurring` setting, the rate card's `proration` and its `billing_timing`. For example, `volume` is arrears only and fixed products accept only `standard`, `graduated` and `volume`. Check the matrix above.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the rate model later?">
    Not on an active rate. Add a new rate with a later `effective_from` and the new model. Pending rates stay fully editable.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
