A spending minimum sets the minimum amount charged for one [rate](/v2/guide/rate-cards/rates) over each of its billing intervals. Use it when a product must bill at least a floor amount per period, whatever the usage.

## How it fits together

- The minimum lives on the rate, in `min_amount_cents`, next to `billing_interval_count` and `billing_interval_unit`.
- It applies per billing interval of that rate. A monthly rate has a minimum per month. A rate billed every three months has one minimum per three-month period.
- It is supported only when the rate card bills in `arrears`. It defaults to `0`.
- It is a rate-level minimum. It is not a customer-wide commitment, and it is not the per-transaction limit of the [percentage](/v2/guide/rate-cards/pricing-models/percentage) model.

## Example

The API call rate of the running example, $0.01 per call, gets a $30 monthly minimum.

| Scenario | Interval | Priced usage | Minimum | Shortfall | Total before tax |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2,000 calls in a full month | 1 month | $20.00 | $30.00 | $10.00 | $30.00 |
| 4,000 calls in a full month | 1 month | $40.00 | $30.00 | $0.00 | $40.00 |
| 2,000 calls in each month of a full quarter | 3 months | $60.00 | $30.00 | $0.00 | $60.00 |

In the first row, the invoice shows the $20.00 usage fee and a $10.00 true-up, which bring the total to $30.00. The true-up fee references its parent fee through `lago_true_up_parent_fee_id`. Do not add the whole $30 minimum on top of usage.

In the third row, the minimum applies once over the three-month period. It is not $30 per month.

These examples use full periods. The baseline example has no minimum and stays at $120.

## Configure the minimum

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Dashboard">
    On the rate card `api_calls_usd`, add or edit a pending rate with these values:
    1. **Model**: standard, **Amount**: 0.01
    2. **Billing interval**: 1 month
    3. **Spending minimum**: 30

    <Note>
    The fields match the API. Screen-by-screen Dashboard steps for v2 are not documented yet.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="API">
    <CodeGroup>
    ```bash Create a rate with a spending minimum
    LAGO_URL="https://api.getlago.com"
    API_KEY="__YOUR_API_KEY__"

    curl --location --request POST "$LAGO_URL/api/v2/rate_cards/api_calls_usd/rates" \
      --header "Authorization: Bearer $API_KEY" \
      --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
      --data-raw '{
        "rate": {
          "code": "calls_with_minimum",
          "effective_from": "2027-07-01T00:00:00Z",
          "rate_model": "standard",
          "rate_properties": { "amount": "0.01" },
          "min_amount_cents": 3000,
          "billing_interval_count": 1,
          "billing_interval_unit": "month"
        }
      }'
    ```
    </CodeGroup>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

`min_amount_cents: 3000` is $30.00 in USD. An active rate rejects changes to `min_amount_cents`. To change the minimum, add a new rate with a later `effective_from`.

## Partial periods

These examples do not cover shortened periods, such as a first cycle that ends on the billing anchor date. Check how the minimum applies to a partial period in a test environment before relying on it.
