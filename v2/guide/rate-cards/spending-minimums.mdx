A spending minimum sets the lowest amount billed for a Rate during one billing period. If the calculated amount is below the minimum, Lago adds a fee for the difference. This additional fee is called a true-up.

## How it fits together

- The minimum lives on the rate, in `min_amount_cents`, next to `billing_interval_count` and `billing_interval_unit`.
- It applies per billing interval of that rate. A monthly rate has a minimum per month. A rate billed every three months has one minimum per three-month period.
- It is supported only when the rate card bills in `arrears`. It defaults to `0`.
- It is a rate-level minimum. It is not a customer-wide commitment, and it is not the per-transaction limit of the [percentage](/v2/guide/rate-cards/pricing-models/percentage) model.

## Example

The API call Rate is 0.01 USD per call, with a 30 USD minimum per billing interval. The examples below cover complete billing periods and show totals for this Rate, not the entire Contract invoice.

| Scenario | Interval | Priced usage | Minimum | Shortfall | Total before tax |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2,000 calls in a full month | 1 month | 20 USD | 30 USD | 10 USD | 30 USD |
| 4,000 calls in a full month | 1 month | 40 USD | 30 USD | 0 USD | 40 USD |
| 2,000 calls in each month of a full quarter | 3 months | 60 USD | 30 USD | 0 USD | 60 USD |

In the first row, the invoice shows the 20 USD usage fee and a 10 USD true-up, which bring the total to 30 USD. The true-up fee references its parent fee through `lago_true_up_parent_fee_id`. Do not add the whole 30 USD minimum on top of usage.

In the third row, the minimum applies once over the three-month period. It is not 30 USD per month.



## Configure the minimum

Spending minimums require a Rate Card with `billing_timing: "arrears"`. Set the minimum when creating a Rate or editing a pending Rate. An active Rate rejects changes to `min_amount_cents`; create a new Rate with a later `effective_from` to change it.

<CodeGroup>
```bash Create a rate with a spending minimum
LAGO_URL="https://api.getlago.com"
API_KEY="__YOUR_API_KEY__"

curl --location --request POST "$LAGO_URL/api/v2/rate_cards/api_calls_usd/rates" \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer $API_KEY" \
  --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --data-raw '{
    "rate": {
      "code": "calls_with_minimum",
      "effective_from": "2027-07-01T00:00:00Z",
      "rate_model": "standard",
      "rate_properties": { "amount": "0.01" },
      "min_amount_cents": 3000,
      "billing_interval_count": 1,
      "billing_interval_unit": "month"
    }
  }'
```
</CodeGroup>

`min_amount_cents: 3000` represents 30 USD.
