A spending minimum sets the lowest amount billed for a Rate during one billing period. If the calculated amount is below the minimum, Lago adds a fee for the difference. This additional fee is called a true-up.

## How it works

A spending minimum applies to one Rate for each of its billing periods. A monthly Rate has a minimum per month. A Rate billed every three months has one minimum for the full three-month period.

The minimum applies to that Rate, rather than the customer's total spending. [Percentage pricing](/v2/guide/rate-cards/pricing-models/percentage) also supports per-transaction limits, which apply to individual transactions.

## Before you start

Spending minimums are available only for Rate Cards billed in arrears, with `billing_timing: "arrears"`. In arrears means billing at the end of the billing period.

You can set the minimum when creating a Rate or updating a pending Rate. To change the minimum after the Rate becomes active, create a new Rate with a later `effective_from`.

## Set the minimum

Set `min_amount_cents` to the minimum amount in cents. It defaults to `0`. For a Rate Card priced in USD, `min_amount_cents: 3000` sets a minimum of 30 USD.

The example below creates a monthly Rate at 0.01 USD per API call with a 30 USD minimum.

<CodeGroup>
```bash Create a rate with a spending minimum
LAGO_URL="https://api.getlago.com"
API_KEY="__YOUR_API_KEY__"

curl --location --request POST "$LAGO_URL/api/v2/rate_cards/api_calls_usd/rates" \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer $API_KEY" \
  --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  --data-raw '{
    "rate": {
      "code": "calls_with_minimum",
      "effective_from": "2027-07-01T00:00:00Z",
      "rate_model": "standard",
      "rate_properties": { "amount": "0.01" },
      "min_amount_cents": 3000,
      "billing_interval_count": 1,
      "billing_interval_unit": "month"
    }
  }'
```
</CodeGroup>

## Example

At 0.01 USD per API call, a Rate with a 30 USD minimum produces the following totals for complete billing periods. These totals cover this Rate only, before tax.

| Usage | Billing period | Usage fee | Minimum | True-up fee | Total before tax |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2,000 calls | 1 month | 20 USD | 30 USD | 10 USD | 30 USD |
| 4,000 calls | 1 month | 40 USD | 30 USD | 0 USD | 40 USD |
| 2,000 calls each month | 3 months | 60 USD | 30 USD | 0 USD | 60 USD |

For 2,000 calls in one month, the usage fee is 20 USD. Lago adds a 10 USD true-up fee to reach the 30 USD minimum.

For a three-month billing period, the 30 USD minimum applies once to the full period. The 60 USD usage fee already exceeds it, so there is no true-up fee.

## Check the result

Compare the usage fee with the minimum for the same Rate and billing period. When the usage fee is below the minimum, check that the true-up fee covers the difference.

In the [fee response](/v2/api-reference/fees/list), `lago_true_up_parent_fee_id` links the true-up fee to its parent fee.
