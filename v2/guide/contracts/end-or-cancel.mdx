title: "End or cancel a contract"
description: "Cancel a pending contract or terminate an active one with DELETE, keep its billing history, and check the final fees and invoices it produced."

Ending a contract stops future service under it while keeping the record and its billing history for reconciliation. A pending contract is canceled. An active contract is terminated.

## Before you end a contract

1. Retrieve the live contract and check its `status` and dates.
2. Decide between a scheduled end and an immediate end. They are not interchangeable:
   - A scheduled end is the contract's `ended_at`. It must be after the start and not already in the past.
   - An immediate end is `DELETE /contracts/{external_id}`.
3. Check the open period in [current usage](/v2/guide/contracts/current-usage) if the contract is active.

## End the contract

`DELETE /contracts/{external_id}` ends the contract lifecycle. It does not delete the record.

| Status before | Status after | Timestamp set |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pending` | `canceled` | `canceled_at` |
| `active` | `terminated` | `terminated_at` |

<Warning>
  Ending a contract is irreversible. A pending contract becomes `canceled` and never starts billing. An active contract becomes `terminated` and stops billing from that point, which can produce final fees. Review the contract and its open usage before you send the request.
</Warning>

## Check final billing

Closing usage and fixed fees depend on timing, the billing window and the rate card's `billing_timing` and `proration`. This guide makes no general claim about a final invoice, refund or proration on termination. Check the records.

1. Retrieve the ended contract with `GET /contracts/{external_id}?status=terminated` (or `status=canceled`).
2. List the customer's [invoices](/v2/guide/invoicing/overview) and inspect their [fees](/v2/guide/invoicing/fees) and service periods.
3. Record the expected and observed amounts.

Existing invoices stay separate financial records. Ending a contract does not by itself prove that an invoice was paid, credited or refunded. To return money, use a [credit note](/v2/guide/invoicing/credit-notes/overview).

```bash Retrieve the terminated contract
LAGO_URL="https://api.getlago.com"
API_KEY="__YOUR_API_KEY__"

curl --location --request GET "$LAGO_URL/api/v2/contracts/contract_example?status=terminated" \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer $API_KEY"
```

After the contract ends, a new contract can use the same `external_id`. Only one pending or active contract can hold it at a time.
