## 1. Is billing that hard for engineers?

Yes. Billing is harder than it first seems. At first, you set a price and customers pay. As the business grows, you deal with dates, price changes, usage tracking, double charges, payment reminders and taxes. Read [why billing systems are a nightmare for engineers](https://getlago.com/blog/why-billing-systems-are-a-nightmare-for-engineers).

Billing intervals alone look simple: weekly, monthly or yearly. In practice you also handle leap years, introductory pricing, proration and the alignment of billing cycles to an anchor date. In Lago, each rate sets its own billing interval, and each applied rate card on a contract follows its own phase timeline.

Lago's API and architecture are open, so you can embed, fork and customize them as your pricing and processes require. [Book a demo](https://getlago.com/book-a-demo) to get started.

## 2. How is Lago different from Stripe? Can Lago replace it?

Lago is an open-source metering and billing platform. Stripe focuses on payment processing and offers billing as one of many products.

Lago can replace Stripe Billing and Stripe Invoicing, but it does not process payments. You keep a payment processor. Lago calculates how much to charge and when, then sends that to the payment provider of your choice. Lago is payment-processor agnostic. Read more on [why Stripe MRR is hard to calculate](https://getlago.com/blog/calculating-stripe-mrr-is-difficult).

## 3. Is Lago cheaper than Stripe?

Stripe Billing is priced as a percentage of billing volume, on top of Stripe Payments fees. Check [Stripe's pricing page](https://stripe.com/pricing) for current rates. Lago does not take a percentage of your billings. Compare the total cost of your billing stack, not one line item.

## 4. How is Lago different from Chargebee?

Chargebee is a good fit for subscription management with standard usage-based components, such as a price per user. Lago is built for usage-based pricing first: event-based metering, metered products, product filters and pricing models such as percentage, graduated percentage and dynamic.

Lago is built for high-volume event ingestion, with default rate limits that can be raised on request. Being open source, Lago also lets engineers audit and extend the code.

## 5. Is Lago cheaper than Chargebee?

Chargebee prices billing by plan tier and a percentage of billing volume above an included amount. Check [Chargebee's pricing page](https://www.chargebee.com/pricing/) for current figures.

Lago offers a free open-source edition for fundamental billing features, and Premium plans with additional features listed on the [Lago pricing page](https://getlago.com/pricing).

## 6. Can Lago be used by non-tech users?

Yes. Lago is developer-first with an interface for non-technical teams.

- **Engineers**: Lago is API-first. It handles high event volumes and fits your existing tools.
- **Product managers**: Model pricing in the product catalog with reusable rate cards, and change prices with effective-dated rates.
- **Finance leaders**: Lago automates fee calculation and invoicing, and keeps invoice status and payment status separate so you can see who paid at a glance.
