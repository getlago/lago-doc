## 1. Does Lago v2 support prepaid credits?

Not in the v2 API yet. The v2 reference does not document wallets or prepaid credits. Prepaid credits remain available with subscriptions. Legacy (v1): [wallets and prepaid credits](/guide/wallet-and-prepaid-credits/overview).

## 2. Which payment methods does Lago support?

Lago is payment-processor agnostic and connects with several [payment providers](/v2/guide/payments/payment-providers). In v2, list a customer's [payment methods](/v2/guide/payments/payment-methods), set the default one, and generate a checkout URL. You can also [record payments made outside Lago](/v2/guide/payments/record-manual-payments).

## 3. Does Lago support invoicing?

Yes. Every plan includes built-in invoicing, without a percentage of billings. With v2 you can:

- Retrieve, update, finalize, refresh and void [invoices](/v2/guide/invoicing/overview)
- Download invoice PDFs and resend invoice emails
- Issue [credit notes](/v2/guide/invoicing/credit-notes/overview)
- Inspect each [fee](/v2/guide/invoicing/fees) and trace it to its product, rate and period
- Attach invoice custom sections to a contract

You can also connect accounting tools such as NetSuite or QuickBooks through [integrations](/v2/integrations/introduction).

## 4. Does Lago provide a customer self-service portal?

The v2 documentation does not cover the customer portal yet. Legacy (v1): [customer portal](/guide/customers/customer-portal).

## 5. Does Lago support different time zones?

Customers have a `timezone` field, which requires a premium license. Contracts set their own cycle boundaries with `started_at` and `billing_anchor_date`. Use explicit timezones in timestamps. Event timestamps are Unix seconds.

## 6. What integrations does Lago support?

Lago offers native integrations for payments (Stripe, GoCardless, Adyen), accounting (NetSuite, QuickBooks), taxes (Anrok, Avalara) and CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot). Check each page in [v2 integrations](/v2/integrations/introduction) for contract support.

## 7. Does Lago support taxation?

Yes. Create taxes with the v2 `/taxes` endpoints and assign them with `tax_codes` on rate cards and plans. Contract current usage applies taxes by default (`apply_taxes=true`). You can also use the [Lago EU Taxes](/v2/integrations/taxes/lago-eu-taxes) or [Anrok](/v2/integrations/taxes/anrok) integrations.

## 8. Does Lago process payments?

No. Lago calculates how much to charge and when, and sends that to your payment provider. You then monitor payment status in Lago. Keep invoice status and payment status separate: a finalized invoice does not by itself mean the customer has paid.

## 9. What is new in v2?

v2 introduces contracts and a product catalog. Read the [contracts FAQ](/v2/faq/contracts) for how contracts, rate cards and the v2 API work.
