Lago Data Pipeline lets you automate the flow of your billing and usage data into your analytics and storage systems.
Use it to run queries in your warehouse or to build dashboards for your finance team from data that stays in sync.

<Note>
Contract-level sync details for V2 are not documented yet. The table list below is the legacy v1 schema. V2 tables (contracts, products, rate cards, rates) are not documented. Check with the Lago team for the V2 table list.
</Note>

## Prerequisites
- **Data sync frequency:** 1 day by default, but we can go down to 15 minutes;
- **Data transfers:** Incremental or Backfill; and
- **Data security:** a high level of security and encryption.
