---
title: "Limitations"
description: "What Quotes & Order Forms does not do. Read this before building a workflow around the feature."
---

Know these boundaries before you build your sales process on Quotes & Order Forms. None of them are final: we build on top of the feature continuously and tackle these limitations based on customer feedback. If one of them blocks your workflow, [tell us](mailto:hello@getlago.com).

**Premium feature.** The feature requires a premium license, with quotes enabled for your organization. [Contact us](mailto:hello@getlago.com) to get access.

**Approval is internal.** Quotes are approved manually, inside Lago, by your own team. There is no customer-facing approval step.

**Lago does not handle signatures.** Order forms are signed outside Lago: DocuSign, Concord, email, or any other tool. You then mark the order form as signed in Lago. The signed file upload is for record-keeping only; Lago does not read or verify it.

**Lago does not send emails.** You share quotes and order forms by downloading the PDF and sending it yourself. There is no public link.

**One currency per quote.** The currency is set at creation and cannot be changed. Every block in the quote uses it. You cannot mix currencies in one quote.

**Amounts are tax-exclusive.** All amounts on quotes, order forms, and orders exclude tax. Taxes are calculated and applied later, at invoicing.

**No native CRM sync.** There is no built-in Salesforce or HubSpot integration for quotes.

**No backdated invoicing.** If the deal's start date is in the past, the subscription is created at that date, but past periods are not invoiced.

**Overrides follow subscription rules.** Price overrides in a quote work exactly like subscription overrides today: you can only override what is already overridable.

**One pricing block per quote.** A quote carries one plan (or, for one-off quotes, one set of add-ons). A subscription amendment covers one plan on one subscription.

**Discounts and credits are for subscription quotes.** One-off quotes cannot include coupons or prepaid credits. A quote can include at most 5 credit blocks.

**Approved means locked.** An approved quote version cannot be edited. To change it, void its order form (which voids the version) and duplicate a new draft. Duplicating a version voids all previous versions.

**Order forms are immutable.** An order form cannot be edited, and when it expires or is voided, its quote version is voided with it.

**Orders are permanent.** An order cannot be voided or cancelled and executes only once. Undoing a deal after execution happens at the billing layer: subscription termination, credit notes.

**One signed file per order form.** The upload accepts a single PDF, JPG, JPEG, or PNG under 10MB, and is no longer possible once the order has been executed.
