## Create a billing entity

Billing entities let you manage your end customers under distinct billing configurations.

To create a billing entity, follow these steps:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Dashboard">
    1. Click **Settings** in the side navigation bar.
    2. In the **Your billing entities** section, click **Add an entity**.
    3. Enter a **name** and a **unique code** for your billing entity.
    4. Configure the entity settings as needed.
    5. Click **Create entity** to confirm.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="API">
    <CodeGroup>
    ```bash Create a billing entity
    LAGO_URL="https://api.getlago.com"
    API_KEY="__YOUR_API_KEY__"

    curl --location --request POST "$LAGO_URL/api/v1/billing_entities" \
      --header "Authorization: Bearer $API_KEY" \
      --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
      --data-raw '{
        "billing_entity": {
          "code": "acme_corp",
          "name": "Acme Corporation",
          "document_number_prefix": "ACM",
          "billing_configuration": {
            "invoice_footer": "Thank you for your business"
          }
        }
      }'
    ```
    </CodeGroup>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Info>
  The first billing entity created in your organization is considered the **default**.

  Billing entity settings apply to the customers assigned to that entity and to the invoices that entity issues. More information is available in the following sections.
</Info>

## Edit a billing entity

You can update all billing entity information **except the code** after creation.

To edit a billing entity:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Dashboard">
    1. Click **Settings** in the side navigation bar.
    2. In the **Your billing entities** section, select the relevant billing entity.
    3. Click any section to edit its settings.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="API">
    <CodeGroup>
    ```bash Update a billing entity
    LAGO_URL="https://api.getlago.com"
    API_KEY="__YOUR_API_KEY__"

    BILLING_ENTITY_CODE="acme_corp"

    curl --location --request PUT "$LAGO_URL/api/v1/billing_entities/$BILLING_ENTITY_CODE" \
      --header "Authorization: Bearer $API_KEY" \
      --header 'Content-Type: application/json' \
      --data-raw '{
        "billing_entity": {
          "name": "Acme Corporation",
          "document_number_prefix": "ACM",
          "billing_configuration": {
            "invoice_footer": "Thank you for your business"
          }
        }
      }'
    ```
    </CodeGroup>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Billing entity settings

A billing entity carries the settings that Lago applies to the invoices it issues:

- Entity details: legal name and number, tax identification number, address, logo and [invoice footer](/guide/invoicing/invoicing-settings/overview#add-a-custom-footer)
- Default currency and timezone
- [Invoice numbering](/guide/invoicing/invoicing-settings/invoice-numbering)
- [Grace period](/guide/invoicing/invoicing-settings/grace-period)
- [Payment terms](/guide/invoicing/invoicing-settings/payment-term)
- [Issuing date preferences](/guide/invoicing/invoicing-settings/issuing_date)
- [Document language](/guide/invoicing/invoicing-settings/overview#translate-invoices)
- [Zero-amount invoices](/guide/invoicing/invoicing-settings/empty-invoices)
- [Taxes](/guide/invoicing/invoicing-settings/taxes)
- [Invoice custom sections](/guide/invoicing/invoicing-settings/overview#add-a-custom-invoice-section)
- [Emails](/guide/emails)
- [E-invoicing](/guide/invoicing/e-invoicing/overview)

Through the API, taxes and invoice custom sections can only be set when you [update a billing entity](/api-reference/billing-entities/update), not when you [create one](/api-reference/billing-entities/create). The [billing entity object](/api-reference/billing-entities/object) lists every field.

## Archive a billing entity

Archiving a billing entity is **not available** in the Dashboard or through the API. In the Dashboard, the **Archive billing entity** action of a billing entity asks you to contact Lago.

<Info>
  To archive a billing entity, contact the Lago team directly.
</Info>

---

## Assign a billing entity to a customer

When creating a customer, you can assign it to a billing entity in two ways:

- Through the Dashboard by selecting the billing entity.
- Via API by passing the `billing_entity_code` in the customer creation payload.

If you do not pass `billing_entity_code`, Lago assigns the customer to the **default** billing entity.

<Info>
  The billing entity assigned to a customer acts as their **default billing entity**.
</Info>

A customer is not limited to a single billing entity. When you create a subscription, a one-off invoice or a wallet for that customer, you can pick a billing entity other than the customer's default. The resulting invoices are issued by the billing entity selected on that object.
