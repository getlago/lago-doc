title: "Finance subscription and invoice workflow"
description: "Start a recurring plan on Lago Cloud, collect through an existing Adyen account, and review invoices as pricing grows."
---

Finance can create subscriptions and review invoices in the Lago app without running the billing service. Lago Cloud hosts the application; your team still defines billing rules, connects Adyen, and supplies the customer and usage data Lago needs.

## Start one recurring plan with Adyen

1. **Set up the plan.** Define a monthly subscription fee, currency, and whether the fee is billed in advance or arrears. A [plan](/guide/plans/overview) becomes billable when you assign it to a customer.
2. **Connect your Adyen account.** Configure the [native Adyen integration](/integrations/payments/adyen-integration) with your merchant account, API key, recurring-payment settings, and webhook. Check the payment methods and capture settings enabled on your Adyen account. Lago initiates collection for eligible invoices; Adyen processes the payment.
3. **Add the customer and collect a payment method.** Set Adyen as the customer's default payment provider. You can link an existing Adyen shopper reference or use Lago's Adyen checkout URL to collect and store a method for a new shopper. The checkout URL performs a zero-value pre-authorization; it does not let the customer choose a Lago plan or create a subscription. Confirm the method is stored and Lago receives Adyen's webhook before relying on automatic collection.
4. **Assign the plan.** In the customer's **Overview** tab, choose **Add a plan**, select the plan and billing date, and confirm. Lago creates the subscription and generates invoices according to the plan's billing cycle. [Customer-specific plan overrides](/guide/subscriptions/assign-plan#overriding-a-plan) require Premium.
5. **Verify the first collection.** Compare the first invoice with the plan and check its payment status against Adyen. If collection fails or no usable method is on file, follow the [payment retry and payment URL steps](/guide/payments/payment-retries) for that invoice.

For self-service signup, your application still has to create or assign the Lago customer and subscription. The Adyen checkout URL above is a payment-method collection step, not a complete hosted subscription signup page.

## Add usage and negotiated terms later

Configure a plan with a [usage-based charge](/guide/plans/charges/usage-based-charges) when the product begins sending billable events, then check the [plan-change timing and invoice behavior](/guide/subscriptions/upgrades-downgrades) before moving existing subscriptions. For customer-specific pricing, Premium [subscription overrides](/guide/subscriptions/assign-plan#overriding-a-plan) can change supported plan amounts and charge properties. [Quotes and order forms](/guide/quotes/overview), available as a Premium add-on on request, can carry signed terms into a new subscription; signatures happen outside Lago. Check the selected Cloud features and Adyen test flow before committing to the rollout. Adyen remains the payment processor as the billing model changes.

## From invoice to reconciliation

1. **Review the invoice.** In **Invoices**, filter by customer, status, issuing date, overdue state, payment status, or billing entity. Open the invoice and compare its subscription, usage fees, tax, discounts, and credits with the agreed terms. Use the [invoice preview](/guide/invoicing/previews) before issue when the Premium preview endpoint fits your workflow.
2. **Check collection.** A [payment-provider integration](/guide/payments/payment-providers) can initiate payment and update the invoice's status. Inspect failed or overdue payments in the [cash collection reports](/guide/analytics/cash-collection/dunnings); payment processing itself happens with the connected provider.
3. **Export what Finance needs.** [Export invoices](/guide/invoicing/export) as one row per invoice or one row per fee. Filter before export and reconcile totals by currency and billing period. The [analytics overview](/guide/analytics/overview) explains report definitions and update timing; its billing metrics are distinct from revenue recognition.
4. **Connect accounting.** Lago documents [NetSuite](/integrations/accounting/netsuite) and [Xero](/integrations/accounting/xero) integrations, each available on request. [QuickBooks](/integrations/accounting/quickbooks) uses a community-maintained n8n workflow. Check the data direction, field mapping, availability, and reconciliation method for your chosen system.
5. **Investigate exceptions.** The **[Finance Assistant (Beta)](/guide/ai-agents/finance-assistant)** can answer read-only questions in the Lago app or through the [Lago MCP Server](/guide/ai-agents/mcp-server) in an MCP-compatible client. Review AI-generated figures against the app, export, or API before using them in financial reporting. Access follows the API key's data scope, so control who can use the assistant.
