---
title: "Negotiated contract billing"
description: "Configure customer-specific pricing and a minimum commitment, then verify the Lago invoice and finance handoff."
---

A negotiated contract can combine a subscription, metered usage, customer-specific prices, credits, and a minimum commitment. The selection test is the actual invoice the contract will produce, including a below-minimum month and an above-minimum month.

## Example: a monthly commitment

Assume a customer agrees to a **$1,000 monthly minimum** for metered usage. With $800 of eligible usage, Lago adds a $200 true-up to reach the minimum. With $1,300 of eligible usage, it bills $1,300 and adds no true-up. These figures are illustrative, before tax, discounts, or credits. [Minimum commitments](/guide/plans/commitment) are calculated on the pre-tax amount before discounts.

| Usage in the month | Usage line | Commitment true-up | Pre-tax amount |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Below minimum | $800 | $200 | $1,000 |
| Above minimum | $1,300 | $0 | $1,300 |

## Configure and verify the contract

1. **Translate the agreement into billing terms.** Record the billing interval, usage metric, unit price, minimum commitment, prepaid credits, payment terms, and amendment rules. [Create a plan](/guide/plans/overview) and [override its terms for this customer](/guide/subscriptions/assign-plan) where the agreement differs from the standard catalog.
2. **Configure the minimum.** Set a [minimum commitment](/guide/plans/commitment) on the plan or override it for the customer. Check which charges count toward the minimum and when the true-up appears.
3. **Choose how sales terms enter Lago.** You can assign and override a plan directly. If your team uses [Quotes & Order Forms](/guide/quotes/overview), it can turn an approved quote and signed order form into an order that provisions billing. Quotes & Order Forms is available on request; confirm access and the required approval roles before designing around it.
4. **Send representative usage and preview the invoice.** Use an isolated test organization, then inspect the Premium [invoice preview](/guide/invoicing/previews) for the configured subscription. Compare both a below-minimum and above-minimum month with the signed terms. A preview uses configured customer and billing data; it is not an arbitrary mock invoice.
5. **Check the handoff.** Verify the finalized invoice, payment method, credit notes if needed, and the [Finance workflow](/guide/lago-cloud/finance-workflow). Confirm the accounting integration and its availability before treating the contract as operationally ready.

For a vendor evaluation, ask each provider to reproduce **the same** contract and explain every invoice line, amendment, approval, and integration step. The work needed to connect your product and finance stack belongs in the comparison alongside software fees.
