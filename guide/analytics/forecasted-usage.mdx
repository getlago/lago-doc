## What's on this dashboard

Two charts. Both are **activity**: each value covers the month it sits in. The forecast runs **12 months ahead**, one value per month.

| Chart | What it answers | Values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forecast units | How much will customers consume each month? | 🔢 Billing units |
| Forecast amount | What is that consumption worth? | 💰 Money (excl. tax) |

## The three scenarios

Every forecast comes in three versions, so you can plan against a range rather than a single number.

| Scenario | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| **Conservative** | A cautious floor. Budgeting, covenant headroom, worst-case planning |
| **Realistic** | The most likely outcome. Your default number |
| **Optimistic** | An upper bound. Capacity planning and best-case modelling |

## What is being forecast

| | Counted? | If not, where is it? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Metered consumption you expect to be **paid** for | ✅ Yes | |
| Expected growth in your subscription count | ✅ Yes | |
| Consumption invoiced but never collected | ❌ No | [Invoices](/guide/analytics/invoices) |
| Subscription fees and one-off charges | ❌ No | [MRR](/guide/analytics/revenue/mrr) |
| Products and features you have not launched yet | ❌ No | Nothing can forecast these |

<Warning>
**The forecast only knows what has already happened.** It learns from your consumption history, so it cannot anticipate a new product, a new pricing model, or a customer you have not signed. Read it as a projection of the current business, not of your plans.
</Warning>

## Filters

| Filter | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Currency | Keeps one billing currency |
| Country | Keeps customers in one country |
| Customer external ID | Isolates a single customer |
| Customer type | Keeps companies or individuals |
| Customer has tax ID | Keeps customers with, or without, a tax ID on file |
| Plan code | Keeps one plan |
| Subscription ext. ID | Isolates a single subscription |
| Billable metric | Keeps one metric. Do this before reading 🔢 units, or you are adding API calls to gigabytes |

⚠️ There is no date range here. The horizon is always the next 12 months.

## What's not included (for now)

- **New products and features.** The forecast is built from what your customers already consume.
- **Recently created metrics and charges.** They may be left out until they have enough history.
- **Unused or deleted metrics and charges.** They may be left out too.
- **Anything other than usage.** Subscription revenue is on [MRR](/guide/analytics/revenue/mrr), invoiced revenue on [Revenue streams](/guide/analytics/revenue/revenue-streams).

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Why don't I see any forecast?">
Forecasting needs at least six months of consumption history to produce something reliable. If your organization has less than that, forecasts appear once enough history has built up.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How often does the forecast change?">
It is refreshed once a month, usually during the first two weeks. It does not move day to day like the other reports.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Which number should I put in the board pack?">
The realistic scenario, and quote the conservative one next to it as your floor. Quoting the optimistic figure alone will overstate the case.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A big metric is missing from the forecast.">
It is probably too new, or it has been unused or deleted. Metrics and charges without enough recent history can be left out. Check its consumption on [Usage](/guide/analytics/usage).
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
