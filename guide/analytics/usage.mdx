---
title: "Usage"
description: "How Lago measures metered consumption and its cost, including usage that has not been invoiced yet."
---

Usage reports what your customers consumed and what that consumption is worth. It answers "who is using the product, on which metrics, and what is it priced at".

This is the one revenue-adjacent dashboard that does not wait for an invoice. Consumption from the current, open billing period is already in it.

## Population

**Included**

- Metered consumption recorded against subscriptions, day by day, priced by the plan's charges.
- Usage from open billing periods, so consumption that has no fee and no invoice yet.
- Consumption broken down to the level of individual charges, charge filters and groups, then aggregated back up for these reports.

**Excluded**

- Subscription fees, one-off charges, commitment true-ups. This dashboard is metered consumption only. For all charged revenue, see [Revenue streams](/guide/analytics/revenue/revenue-streams).
- Taxes.

<Note>
Usage is dated on the **usage date in the customer's timezone**, not in your organization's timezone. A customer in Tokyo and a customer in Los Angeles consuming at the same moment can land on different days.
</Note>

## Total usage cost

What the consumption is worth at plan prices.

**Included** The priced amount of metered consumption in the period, including consumption not yet invoiced.

**Excluded** Discounts, credits and commitment true-ups, which are applied at invoicing. The figure here is consumption at list price for the customer's plan.

**How it's calculated** The sum of daily priced usage in the period.

**Grain and timing** Flow, dated on the usage date. Adds correctly across periods.

**Currency** Reported in the plan's currency, never converted.

## Total units consumed

How much was consumed, in the units your metrics measure.

**Included** The metered units recorded in the period, per billable metric.

**Excluded** Nothing beyond the population rules. Units are raw consumption, before any pricing.

**How it's calculated** The sum of daily units in the period.

**Grain and timing** Flow, dated on the usage date.

**Currency** Not applicable. Units are counts, not money.

<Warning>
**Units are not comparable across billable metrics.** With no breakdown applied, this chart adds API calls to gigabytes to seats. Break down by billable metric, or filter to one, before quoting a number.
</Warning>

## Usage breakdown

A pivot table of the same two measures, cut by the dimensions you select. Use it to find which metric, customer or plan drove a spike, then confirm it against the day-level detail.

## Breakdown and filters

The Breakdown selector cuts the reports by **Billable metric**, **Currency**, **Customer** and **Plan**. A dedicated **Billable metric** filter narrows the whole dashboard to one metric, which is the fastest way to make units meaningful.

## Reconciliation and common questions

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Why is usage cost higher than the usage-based revenue I invoiced?">
Several reasons, and they compound. Consumption from an open billing period has no invoice yet. Usage absorbed by a minimum commitment is billed as a commitment fee, not as usage. Usage paid with prepaid credits shows full gross revenue and an offsetting contra line on the invoice. Coupons reduce the invoice, not the usage cost here.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use this to explain an invoice a customer is disputing?">
Yes, and it is the fastest route. Filter to that subscription and billable metric, set granularity to day, and set the date range to the invoice's billing period. The day-by-day series shows when consumption spiked and what it priced at. The [usage API](/guide/events/retrieve-usage) returns the same breakdown programmatically.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A customer says they consumed nothing yesterday, but the chart shows usage.">
Check the timezone. Usage is dated in the customer's timezone, so late-evening consumption can land on the following day relative to your own clock. Also check the refresh: the analytical data updates daily, so today's consumption may be partial.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does deleted or voided billing data change these numbers?">
Usage is measured from consumption, not from invoices, so voiding an invoice does not change it. Terminating a subscription stops future usage but leaves past consumption in place.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
