When a new invoice is generated, Lago also sends an `invoice.created` webhook
([learn more](/api-reference/webhooks/messages)).

When an invoice is linked to an add-on, another type of webhook is sent, called
`invoice.one_off_created`.

## Invoice object

Invoices include (but are not limited to):

- Invoice number;
- Billing period;
- Customer information;
- Fees; and
- Taxes.

You can [download invoices](/guide/invoicing/download-invoices) or use the
[invoice object](/api-reference/invoices/invoice-object) to create your own invoice
template.

For payment collection, connect a [native payment provider](/guide/payments/payment-providers). For accounting, compare the [NetSuite](/integrations/accounting/netsuite) and [Xero](/integrations/accounting/xero) integrations, which are available on request, with the [community-maintained QuickBooks integration](/integrations/accounting/quickbooks). You can also use invoice webhooks and the API for a custom workflow.

If you are evaluating the full process, follow the [Finance invoice workflow](/guide/lago-cloud/finance-workflow) from invoice review through payment status, export, and accounting handoff.
