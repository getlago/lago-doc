Lago calculates subscription and usage charges, generates invoices, and connects billing to payment providers and finance tools. [Lago Cloud](/guide/lago-cloud) is the hosted option: Lago runs and updates the billing service while your team configures its pricing and connects customer and usage data. You can also [self-host Lago](/guide/lago-self-hosted/overview) when your team needs to operate the deployment.

For enterprise customers, configure [negotiated prices](/guide/subscriptions/assign-plan#overriding-a-plan) and [minimum spending commitments](/guide/plans/commitment). For prepaid usage, [wallets](/guide/wallet-and-prepaid-credits/overview#real-time-wallet-balance) track an ongoing balance that typically refreshes in under a second after a new event is included in usage. Connect [Salesforce CRM](/integrations/crm/salesforce-crm), [Salesforce CPQ](/integrations/crm/salesforce-cpq), or [NetSuite](/integrations/accounting/netsuite) to bring billing into your sales and accounting workflows; these integrations are available on request.

## Five-step billing workflow

### 1. Usage ingestion

[Events](/guide/events/ingesting-usage) provide information about your customers'
consumption, with the highest level of granularity. Any product feature for
which you want to charge your customers should correspond to a specific event.
An event can be triggered by an action or can be sent periodically. Lago ingests
events at scale while preventing duplicates.

### 2. Metrics aggregation

The aggregation process consists of converting events into
[billable metrics](/guide/billable-metrics/create-billable-metrics). There are 6
[aggregation types](/guide/billable-metrics/aggregation-types/overview):

| Aggregation    | Description                                                                                            | Type                 |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------- |
| `COUNT`        | Counts the number of times an incoming event occurs                                                    | Metered              |
| `COUNT UNIQUE` | Returns the number of unique values of a predefined property for incoming events                       | Metered or Recurring |
| `LATEST`       | Returns the latest value of a predefined property for incoming events                                  | Metered              |
| `MAX`          | Returns the maximum value of a predefined property for incoming events                                 | Metered              |
| `SUM`          | Calculates the sum of a predefined property for incoming events                                        | Metered or Recurring |
| `WEIGHTED SUM` | Calculates the sum of a predefined property for incoming events prorated based on time used per period | Metered or Recurring |

### 3. Pricing and packaging

[Plans](/guide/plans/overview) define how much your customers pay for using your
product. Combine plans and billable metrics to implement any billing model:
subscription-based, usage-based, or hybrid. Billable metrics may be priced
differently depending on the associated plan.

### 4. Invoicing

Lago automatically generates [invoices](/guide/invoicing/overview) for each customer
according to the plan model: the subscription fee can be billed at the beginning
or at the end of the billing period, and each usage-based charge is billed
`in advance` or `in arrears` depending on how you configure it. Invoices
include fees, taxes and customer information.

### 5. Payments

When an invoice is emitted and finalized, it's ready for payment collection.
Connect payment providers through [native integrations](/guide/payments/payment-providers) or
trigger payments on any PSP using the invoice payload created by Lago.
