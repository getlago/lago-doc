---
title: "Edit a subscription"
description: "Update negotiated prices, commitments, subscription dates, and payment terms for an individual customer through the dashboard or API."
---

Once a plan is assigned to a customer, you can edit a pending or active subscription through the dashboard or API. On [Lago Cloud](/guide/lago-cloud) or Lago Self-Hosted Premium, use [plan overrides](/guide/subscriptions/assign-plan#overriding-a-plan) to update that customer's negotiated charges, tiers, spending minimums, and [minimum commitment](/guide/plans/commitment).

An override customizes the subscription without changing the shared parent plan. To move the customer to a different plan, follow [upgrades and downgrades](/guide/subscriptions/upgrades-downgrades), which explains the timing and proration for that transition.

<Info>
A subscription held `incomplete` by an [activation rule](/guide/subscriptions/activation-rules) cannot be edited, upgraded or downgraded until it activates or is canceled.
</Info>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Dashboard">
  1. Click the three dots menu icon and select **"Edit subscription"**;
  2. **Subscription start date**   
  You can modify the subscription start date if the subscription is pending and the chosen date is later than today. See the [guidelines for subscription start date](/guide/subscriptions/assign-plan#subscription-start-date);
  3. **Subscription end date**   
  You can also change the subscription end date. See the [guidelines for subscription end date](/guide/subscriptions/assign-plan#subscription-end-date); and
  4. You can edit any prices listed for a subscription, including charges, commitments, tiers and spending minimums.
  5. In **Invoicing settings**, you can [change the subscription's payment term](#change-payment-terms) or restore inheritance from the customer.
  </Tab>

<Info>
  Fixed charges are updated under `plan_overrides`, using the `fixed_charges` array. Sending only `units` (and `apply_units_immediately` if needed) updates the quantity without creating a plan override. Passing a price or `properties` creates one. See [Update a fixed charge via the API](/guide/plans/charges/fixed-charges#update-a-fixed-charge-via-the-api).
</Info>

## Review and reconcile negotiated changes

Before changing an active customer's prices or commitment:

1. Identify the subscription and its current overridden plan with the [retrieve a subscription API](/api-reference/subscriptions/get-specific).
2. Review the agreed prices, commitment interval, and dates. For annual minimum spend billed alongside monthly usage, see the [worked commitment example](/guide/plans/commitment#annual-commitment-with-monthly-usage-invoices).
3. Reproduce the proposed update in a [separate test organization](/guide/integration-testing). Compare [dry-run invoices](/guide/invoicing/previews#dry-run-invoices-for-active-subscriptions) before and after the edit, including usage on either side of the agreed amendment date, before changing the production subscription.
4. After applying the change, reconcile [current and past usage](/guide/events/retrieve-usage) with the resulting invoice lines. Use [invoice exports](/guide/invoicing/export) or the [NetSuite integration](/integrations/accounting/netsuite) for your accounting workflow.

If sales manages negotiated offers in Salesforce, the [Salesforce CPQ integration](/integrations/crm/salesforce-cpq#iii-actions-from-salesforce-to-lago) provides configurable flows to assign a plan, set negotiated prices, and start a subscription when a quote is accepted.

## Change payment terms

On the subscription overview, the current term appears under **Invoicing settings**, with an inheritance hint when the subscription has none of its own. Click **Edit**, choose the payment-term type and its days or month settings, then save. To return to the customer's default, choose the inheritance option. See [Payment terms](/guide/invoicing/invoicing-settings/payment-term#choose-a-payment-term) for the available options and examples.

For example, change a subscription from **Net 30** to **Net 60** after negotiating a longer payment window. A new invoice dated **April 10, 2026** is due **June 9** instead of **May 10**. The customer's other subscriptions keep their own or inherited terms.

Existing invoices, including drafts, keep the payment term they were created with. Different effective terms also affect [whether subscriptions can share an invoice](/guide/subscriptions/assign-plan#invoice-consolidation).
