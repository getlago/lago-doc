## Receipt object

Payment receipts include (but are not limited to):

- Receipt number;
- Payment date;
- Customer information;
- Payment amount;
- Remaining due payment amount;
- Reference to impacted invoices; and
- Fees, taxes and total amount.

You can **download receipts** or use the receipt object to create your own receipt template.

## Multiple receipts per invoice

An invoice can have multiple receipts associated with it, especially in cases where:

- Partial payments are made over time; or
- Manual payments are recorded alongside automatic payments.

You can therefore track every payment made towards an invoice and keep a clear audit trail of the payment history.

<Tip>
  For accounting and compliance purposes, Lago maintains the integrity of finalized invoices by never modifying them. Instead, payment receipts track all payment activities and remaining balances.
</Tip>

## Downloading receipts

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Dashboard">
    You can download payment receipts directly from the payment details page in the Lago dashboard.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="API">
    List the receipts of an invoice, then download each one from the `file_url` of the receipt object. The receipt also carries `xml_url` for its e-invoicing file.

    <CodeGroup>
    ```bash List the payment receipts of an invoice
    LAGO_URL="https://api.getlago.com"
    API_KEY="__YOUR_API_KEY__"
    INVOICE_ID="__YOUR_INVOICE_ID__"

    curl --location --request GET "$LAGO_URL/api/v1/payment_receipts?invoice_id=$INVOICE_ID" \
      --header "Authorization: Bearer $API_KEY"
    ```
    </CodeGroup>

    Both links expire. `file_url_expires_at` and `xml_url_expires_at` give the moment they stop working, so read the receipt again to get fresh links.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Receipt email notifications

When the payment receipt email scenario is on, Lago emails the payment receipt and the corresponding invoices to the customer every time a payment is processed. To turn it on:

1. Go to **Settings** in the Lago dashboard;
2. Select the **Email scenarios** tab;
3. Find the **Payment receipt** email scenario; and
4. Toggle the switch on.

See [Emails](/guide/emails) for the prerequisites that apply to every email scenario.
