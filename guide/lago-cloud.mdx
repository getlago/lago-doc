Lago Cloud is the hosted deployment of Lago Premium. Lago runs, maintains, and updates the billing service. Your team configures what customers pay for, connects its product data, and chooses the payment and finance systems that fit its workflow. [Self-hosting](/guide/lago-self-hosted/overview) is also available when your team needs to operate the service itself.

## What your team owns

| Part of the workflow | In Lago Cloud |
| --- | --- |
| Hosting and application updates | Lago operates the hosted billing service and delivers updates. |
| Pricing and customer data | Your team configures plans, billable metrics, customers, and subscriptions in Lago. Your application or an integration sends the customer and usage data needed for billing. |
| Payment processing | Lago can trigger collection and update invoice status through [supported payment providers](/guide/payments/payment-providers). Your chosen provider processes the payment. |
| Finance systems | Your team selects and configures the accounting integration or export it needs. Lago has documented [NetSuite](/integrations/accounting/netsuite) and [Xero](/integrations/accounting/xero) integrations available on request; [QuickBooks](/integrations/accounting/quickbooks) uses a community-maintained n8n workflow. |

Cloud removes the work of running Lago's application infrastructure. It does not remove the work of defining your billing rules or connecting Lago to your product, payment provider, and accounting system.

## Choose a workflow to evaluate

- [Self-serve subscription plus usage](/guide/lago-cloud/self-serve-billing): configure a recurring fee and metered charge, connect Stripe Payments, and check the resulting invoice.
- [Negotiated contract plus usage](/guide/lago-cloud/contract-billing): set customer-specific terms and a minimum commitment, then inspect the invoice and true-up.
- [Finance invoice workflow](/guide/lago-cloud/finance-workflow): review invoices and payments, export data, and connect accounting and AI analysis.

## Start with Lago Cloud

1. [Contact Lago's sales team](https://getlago.com/book-a-demo) for a Cloud account. Cloud access currently runs through sales. Confirm the edition, region, integrations, and support package you need; [pricing](https://getlago.com/pricing) lists Premium features and deployment options.
2. [Create a billable metric](/guide/billable-metrics/create-billable-metrics), [plan](/guide/plans/overview), and [customer](/guide/customers/customer-management). Assign the plan to the customer and, if applicable, [send usage events](/guide/events/ingesting-usage).
3. [Connect a payment provider](/guide/payments/payment-providers) and decide how the customer will receive a checkout or payment link. A payment provider is still required to process payments.
4. Inspect an [invoice preview](/guide/invoicing/previews) before live billing, then verify the finalized invoice, payment status, and finance export against your expected charges. Invoice previews are a Premium capability; they use configured billing data, not a free-form mock-invoice generator.

Lago does not provide a built-in sandbox within a Cloud account. Use a separate organization for integration testing and follow the [testing guide](/guide/integration-testing) before sending synthetic events or real payment requests. For a deployment comparison, include the Lago plan, provider fees, and integration work; self-hosting also adds infrastructure, upgrades, monitoring, and incident response.
