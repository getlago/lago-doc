If you’re selling your product or service through a partner, model this revenue-share in Lago.
Mark your customer as a partner, and Lago generates all their invoices as self-billed invoices.

Self-billing is a billing arrangement where the customer creates and issues the invoice on behalf of the supplier for goods or services received.
Ensure an agreement is in place with your partner so these invoices can be issued on their behalf.

## Create a partner
To generate self-billed invoices, create your first partner by following these steps:
<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Dashboard">
    1. Go to the **Customers** navigation tab.
    2. Click on **Create a customer**.
    3. Turn on **Mark this customer as a partner**.
    4. Fill in the customer’s billing information. It is used to generate invoices.

      <img src="/guide/images/customer-partner.png" alt="The Create a customer dialog with the Mark this customer as a partner toggle turned on" />
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="API">

## Generate invoices
To simplify your payment process, Lago generates invoices on behalf of partners.

Every invoice generated for a partner is marked as **self-billed**, so it is left out of [Revenue streams](/guide/analytics/revenue/revenue-streams). It still appears on [Invoices](/guide/analytics/invoices).

The PDF is titled **Self-billing invoice** and ignores your custom footer.
It also carries a notice stating that the invoice was generated on behalf of the partner with their consent.

Self-billed invoices always use the per-customer [invoice numbering](/guide/invoicing/invoicing-settings/invoice-numbering), even when the billing entity is set to entity-wide numbering.
