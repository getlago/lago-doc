On Lago Cloud self-serve, each production account has one linked sandbox. You build and test your pricing in the sandbox, then use **Copy to production** to copy each item to production.

The copy happens once. The item keeps its code in production, and your sandbox keeps its own version. Later changes in the sandbox never reach production, and nothing is ever copied from production to the sandbox.

## Before you start

- **You must be an admin of the production account.** For anyone else, the copy is refused with "Only admins can copy items to production."
- **Production must be active.** Add a card to your production account first. Until then, every copy is refused.
- **Copy to production is only available in the sandbox.** Production items have no such action.

## What you can copy

| Item | What is copied |
| --- | --- |
| Billable metric | The metric with its filters. |
| Feature | The feature with its privileges. |
| Coupon | The coupon, and the billable metrics it is limited to. |
| Product category | The category with all its products. |
| Product | The product with its filters and rate cards. Its category must be in production first. |
| Plan | The whole plan: its rate cards with their phases, its entitlements, its wallets (with their credit allocations and alerts) and its taxes. |
| Plan rate card | One rate card of a plan, with its phases. The plan must be in production first. |

No other item has the action. Customers, subscriptions, events, invoices and add-ons stay in the sandbox.

When a rate card is copied, its current rate starts applying in production at the time of the copy. Rates scheduled for later are kept, and past rates are not copied.

## Copy an item

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the item's actions">
    In your sandbox, open the list of items (for example **Billable metrics**) and click the actions menu of the item, or open the item's detail page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Copy to production">
    A confirmation window asks you to confirm the copy of the item and all its child items.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Copy">
    Lago copies the item and everything it needs in one step. When the copy succeeds, you see a message such as "`storage` copied to production.", with a link to production. If anything fails, a message says why, and nothing is copied.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The production [activity log](/guide/security/audit-logs) records each item the copy creates, for example `billable_metric.copied_to_production`, under the name of the admin who copied it.

## Codes already in production

Lago matches items by code, and never overwrites a production item.

- **The item itself:** when production already has an item with the same code, the copy is refused, for example with "An active item already exists for plan `premium`". To change an item in production, edit it there.
- **The items it uses:** when production already has a related item with the same code, Lago reuses it as it is, without changing it. Otherwise Lago copies it along.

Related items that are reused or copied along include billable metrics, taxes, pricing units, features, product categories, products and rate cards. For example, copying a plan whose billable metric is already in production reuses that metric and copies the plan's other metrics.

## Parents go first

Some items belong to another item, which must be in production before you copy them:

- a product needs its product category;
- a plan rate card needs its plan.

Until then the copy is refused, for example with "Copy the plan `premium` to production first." Copying a product category or a plan copies its products or rate cards along, so you do not need to copy them one by one.

## Copying a rate card into a plan in production

You can add a sandbox rate card to a plan that is already in production. Lago copies only that rate card and its phases into that plan.

A plan in production that has subscriptions cannot change its pricing, so the copy is refused in that case.

## Why a copy can be refused

When a copy is refused, a message tells you why, and nothing is copied. The table lists each reason and what to do.

| Reason | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The coupon is terminated or expired. | Create a new coupon in the sandbox, or create it in production. |
| The coupon is limited to plans. | Create the coupon in production. |
| The item relies on deleted items: a coupon whose billable metrics are all deleted, a product whose billable metric is deleted, or a plan wallet whose targets are all deleted. | Point the item to items that still exist. |
| The billable metric uses a custom aggregation. | Recreate the metric in production. |
| Production already uses the code for another item, for example a product in another category or a rate card in another product. | Change the code in your sandbox. |
| An item to reuse in production lacks a filter the item uses: a billable metric without a filter value of the product, or a product without a filter of the rate card or plan wallet. | Add the filter in production. |
| A feature to reuse in production lacks a privilege the plan uses, or has one with another value type or without a select option the plan uses. | Add or change the privilege in production. |
| A prorated rate card would bill a billable metric in production that is not recurring, or that uses a weighted sum. | Use a recurring metric without a weighted sum in production. |
| A rate card's pricing model, or a phase's rate, does not suit the product or billable metric it lands on in production. | Change the pricing model in your sandbox. |
| The plan in production has subscriptions, so its pricing cannot change. | Create a new plan, or change the plan in production. |
| The plan in production already prices the same product, or product filter, with another rate card. | Remove the duplicate pricing in your sandbox or in production. |
| The rate card's currency differs from the plan's. | Use a rate card in the plan's currency. |
| A pricing unit to copy needs a premium license that production does not have. | Contact the Lago team. |
