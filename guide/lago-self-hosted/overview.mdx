You can host Lago on your local computer, on a dedicated server, or in the cloud. Your team operates the application, its databases, upgrades, and monitoring. To skip operating the stack, use [Lago Cloud](/guide/lago-cloud).

## Editions

| Edition | License | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open source | [AGPL-3.0](https://github.com/getlago/lago) | Free. Deploy from the public repository. |
| Premium | Commercial | [Contact Lago](https://getlago.com/book-a-demo). Features and support differ by edition; see [pricing](https://getlago.com/pricing). |
