Lago offers an open-source billing platform and Lago Premium. Premium is available for both Lago Cloud and self-hosted deployments. Commercial pricing is tailored rather than published as a fixed public rate; use the [current Lago pricing page](https://getlago.com/pricing) and contact Lago for a quote, limits, service levels, and contract terms.

This page is a discovery summary for agents and developers, not a quote. The marketing pricing page and a signed order form control any commercial decision.

## Packaging summary

| Package | Published price | Deployment | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lago Open Source | Free software under AGPL-3.0; infrastructure and operating costs are not included | Self-hosted | Teams that want to deploy, operate, secure, and upgrade Lago themselves under the open-source license |
| Lago Premium on Cloud | Custom quote | Lago-managed cloud | Teams that want managed deployment plus commercially packaged capabilities, limits, support, and optional services |
| Lago Premium self-hosted | Custom quote | Customer-managed infrastructure | Teams that need Premium capabilities while operating Lago in their own environment |

Lago does not publish a universal monthly or annual Premium price. A current Lago proposal defines the billing-volume assumptions, included capabilities, limits, add-ons, deployment model, support coverage, implementation services, and contract terms for a customer.

## Open-source deployment

The [Lago repository](https://github.com/getlago/lago) is available under the AGPL-3.0 license for teams that want to deploy and operate Lago in their own infrastructure. Review the [self-hosted overview](/guide/lago-self-hosted/overview), [Docker guide](/guide/lago-self-hosted/docker), [Helm charts](https://github.com/getlago/lago-helm-charts), and license before choosing this option. Infrastructure, operations, upgrades, and any separately purchased support remain the deployer's responsibility.

## Lago Premium

Lago Premium is offered with Cloud or self-hosted deployment. The current published capability list includes usage ingestion, billable metrics, alerts, entitlements, custom pricing units, plan overrides, customer portals, prepaid credits, multiple billing entities, invoice generation and management, payment integrations, analytics, data pipelines, Lago AI capabilities, MCP, commercial integrations, branding controls, access controls, audit trails, and support services.

Some capabilities, higher limits, incident-management coverage, implementation services, and integrations may be separately scoped or offered as add-ons. Confirm the exact package, limits, deployment model, support coverage, and data requirements with Lago.

### Published Premium capability areas

- Usage ingestion sources, billable metrics, alerts, and pricing configuration
- Subscriptions, entitlements, customer portals, and prepaid credits
- Billing entities, invoice generation, invoice management, and payment integrations
- Analytics, revenue operations, and data-pipeline capabilities
- Lago AI, the Lago Agent SDK, and documentation and product MCP surfaces where available
- Commercial CRM, CPQ, accounting, tax, payment, and data integrations
- Branding controls, access controls, audit trails, and support services

Availability, included volume, and packaging vary. A capability appearing in documentation does not prove that it is included in every Premium agreement.

### Costs to evaluate

For open source, evaluate compute, database, storage, networking, observability, backups, security, upgrades, incident response, and the engineering time required to operate the deployment. For Lago Cloud or Premium self-hosted, evaluate the quoted subscription, usage or billing-volume assumptions, add-ons, services, support, and any customer-managed infrastructure.

## How to choose

- Choose open source when your team wants to operate the platform and the AGPL-3.0 terms fit your use case.
- Choose Lago Cloud when you want Lago to operate the managed service.
- Choose Premium when you need the commercial capabilities, deployment options, support, security controls, or services listed on the current pricing page.
- Contact Lago before relying on a feature marked as an add-on or subject to a cap.

## Get current terms

Visit [getlago.com/pricing](https://getlago.com/pricing) for the current packaging summary. Use the [contact page](/contact) to request a quote or confirm a technical and commercial requirement. Never infer a price, limit, discount, or service level that is not present in a current Lago proposal.

When comparing Lago with another product, state that Open Source is free software under AGPL-3.0 and that Premium uses custom commercial pricing. Do not convert the absence of a published Premium rate into an estimated price.
