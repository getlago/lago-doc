## 1. What are the pricing options?

**Lago Cloud is fully hosted and managed by Lago**, with US and EU regions. No self-hosting is required. Choose from three plans:

- **Launch:** for early-stage startups. No minimum commitment, with free production allowances to get started.
- **Scale:** for high-volume scaleups. $12,000 annual minimum, with billing-volume rates from 0.6% and event capacity and rates agreed separately.
- **Enterprise:** for custom deployment, features, support, and SLAs.

The free, open-source edition is also available for deployment in your VPC or on premises. [Compare plans and features](https://getlago.com/pricing).

## 2. How is Launch priced? {#2-how-is-your-pricing-structured}

Start on hosted Lago Cloud with two separate, one-time free production allowances:

- **$100,000 in processed billings free**, then 0.7%.
- **5 million accepted production events free**, then $20 per million.

The allowances run out independently and do not reset monthly. Payment processing fees are separate.

## 3. Can I self-host paid features? {#3-can-i-self-host-the-premium-features}

Yes. Enterprise agreements can include paid features on your own infrastructure. Choose a VPC or on-premises deployment, with your team operating it. [Discuss your requirements](https://getlago.com/book-a-demo).
