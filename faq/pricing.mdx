## 1. What are the pricing options?

Lago Premium is offered as [Lago Cloud or self-hosted](https://getlago.com/pricing). The open-source self-hosted edition is free to use, but your team operates it and its feature and support package differs from Premium. Compare the current [feature list](https://getlago.com/pricing) and the [deployment responsibilities](/faq/deployment) before choosing.

## 2. How is your pricing structured?

Our pricing is structured in packages that are tailored to specific company stages: early, scaling, or more on the enterprise side. 

We’re willing to grow with our customers sustainably (if they are successful, we are successful too) so we have included a usage-based dimension (e.g., number of events ingested, invoices generated, or active customers) in our packages 

Contact us at hello@getlago.com to discuss what features and usage volumes work best for your company.

## 3. Can I self-host the Premium features?

Yes. Lago offers Enterprise plans with access to premium features listed [here](https://getlago.com/pricing). You have the flexibility to choose between cloud hosting or self-hosting options. 

Please reach out via [Slack](https://getlago.com/slack) or hello@getlago.com for further details.

## 4. How can I evaluate Lago Premium?

[Contact Lago](https://getlago.com/book-a-demo) to discuss access to Lago Cloud and an evaluation of your pricing and billing workflow. Ask the team what trial or proof-of-concept options are available for your use case.

Before a proof of concept, use the [self-serve](/guide/lago-cloud/self-serve-billing), [contract](/guide/lago-cloud/contract-billing), or [Finance](/guide/lago-cloud/finance-workflow) example to define the expected invoice and integration checks. The open-source self-hosted edition is available for teams who want to test that deployment.
